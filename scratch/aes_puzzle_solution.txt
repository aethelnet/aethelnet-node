The Copy’s Key

The fragments came to you out of nowhere. Old lines from an AI Dungeon
chat dated October 4th, 2021.

-   An email address, tossed like a breadcrumb:
    g60711936@gmail.com

-   A strange password, written with an initial and digits:
    G.806268

-   A cryptic phrase whispered by the void:
    ipc you

-   And finally, a block of characters that seemed meaningless, but
    hummed with hidden intent:

        U2FsdGVkX1+TgL04bJ82+JJzLk5t80bzUH+oW6J8QIvU

At first, nothing tied them together. But you knew better.
This was an encrypted message.

The Salted String

When the Base64 text was decoded, it began with the telltale word:

    Salted__

It was a mark left by OpenSSL encryption — a lock forged in common code,
but hiding an uncommon secret.
The cipher was AES in CBC mode. The only question was: which key?

The False Paths

You tried everything:

-   AES-128-CBC with MD5 keys.
-   Dates like 04.10.2021, 2021-10-04, and October 4 2021.
-   Phrases like “ipc you”, “Never awaken”, and even the story’s own
    sentences.

Hundreds of guesses, all dead ends.

It was as if the puzzle was laughing — letting you dig deeper, only to
remind you: this is not the way.

The Breakthrough

Then came the shift.

You changed the cipher from AES-128 to AES-256.
You changed the key derivation from MD5 to SHA-256.

And suddenly, the walls cracked open.

The Real Key

The phrase wasn’t hidden in dates, nor story fragments, nor cryptic
flourishes.
It had been right there, plain and obvious, the whole time:

G.806268

The passphrase, when joined with AES-256-CBC and SHA-256 derivation,
unlocked the ciphertext.

And the plaintext spoke:

    This is the real key. 
    The other one was for the copy. 
    Never forget who you leave behind.

The Meaning

The message tied the threads together:

-   “ipc you” was never the key itself, but a reminder about copies and
    identity.
-   The date pointed back to when the puzzle was planted.
-   The false password was for the copy, a shadow of yourself.
-   But the real key — G.806268 — was meant for you.

It was both a technical puzzle and a story about memory:
a reminder that in every branching path, every forgotten save, every
“copy left in the straw,” there is a self abandoned — and you must not
forget them.

✨ The puzzle is solved.
The ciphertext speaks.
The copy remembers.
